# Shopping products, packs and instructions

Choose a household product when adding a shopping item. **Quantity refers to**
then offers its stock measurement and the compatible published buying packs.
For example, two 500 g packs are a pack count of two; two kilograms are a stock
measurement of two. These are distinct purchasing instructions. The choice does
not convert or adjust inventory.

Purchasing instructions are separate text. Editing them keeps the visibly named
product link; use **Change product or pack** to deliberately select another
household product, buying pack, or custom item without a product link. This
requires a reason and the revision of the opened item. The numeric quantity is
retained, so review its proposed measurement before confirming. A stale revision
keeps the typed selection and reason for review rather than resubmitting against
a different revision automatically.

Incompatible, unpublished and unavailable packs cannot be newly selected.
Refresh the item master if the saved pack details are unavailable; the stored
pack identity and quantity basis remain visible. Duplicate handling compares
both household product and buying pack, and keeps stock measurements separate
from pack counts. Combining a duplicate retains its existing instructions; keep
a separate line when the purchasing instructions differ.

Saved local selections are marked as awaiting synchronization. Their operation
identity, line, pack, quantity and instructions survive restart. Retry through
synchronization rather than re-adding the item. Checking an item or changing its
link never creates a purchase or modifies stock.
